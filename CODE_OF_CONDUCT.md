# Code of Conduct

Orka follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). It applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and wherever someone represents the project.

## In short

- Be respectful and assume good intent, including when you disagree.
- Criticize code and ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, sexual content, or personal attacks.
- Do not publish anyone's private information without their permission.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers at **hello@enophiastudio.com**. Every report is reviewed and handled confidentially. Security problems go through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may warn or temporarily or permanently ban contributors, following the Contributor Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
